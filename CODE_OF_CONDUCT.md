# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

KeyVault Sidekick is an open, welcoming project. Everyone who takes part —
filing issues, opening pull requests, asking questions, or reporting security
problems — is expected to help keep it a respectful and welcoming experience
for all, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Assume good faith and give constructive, actionable feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences, and gracefully accept
  constructive criticism.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment of any kind, personal attacks, and disruptive or disrespectful
conduct are not welcome anywhere in the project. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behavior they judge to be inappropriate,
threatening, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests, and discussions — and when an individual is officially representing
the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **rob.lauzon@vibeprosoft.com**. All reports
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the
person reporting will be respected.
